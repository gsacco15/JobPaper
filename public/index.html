JobPaper

Notes and photos in. A PDF you can send out.

JobPaper is a free ChatGPT plugin for small trade crews. Tell ChatGPT about the job and JobPaper turns it into a professional estimate, change order, or daily/weekly job report.

  • “Write up an estimate for a bathroom remodel…”
  • “The customer added a heated floor — change order.”
  • “End of day report for the Henderson job” with your photos.

The document opens as an editable panel that looks like the PDF your customer gets. Tap any line to fix it, then tap Download PDF or Copy as text. Totals, markup, and tax are calculated for you. Estimates are saved as files in your chat, so a change order can pull the original total weeks later.

Get started

In ChatGPT, open Plugins, search for JobPaper, and add it. Then add your business name and phone in JobPaper’s settings so they print on every PDF.
