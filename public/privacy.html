JobPaper

Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

JobPaper is a ChatGPT plugin that turns a contractor’s notes and photos into estimates, change orders, and job reports. This policy explains what JobPaper handles, why, and for how long. It is short because JobPaper keeps very little.

What JobPaper handles

DataWhyKept on our servers?
Job text — descriptions, line items, client name and address you type, notesTo build the document you asked forNo. Processed to build the document and returned to ChatGPT. Not stored or logged.
Photos you attach to a job reportTo show them in the report and PDFNo. JobPaper only receives links to photos you attached. Photos are displayed and placed in the PDF on your device. JobPaper never fetches, searches for, or stores images.
Business settings — business name, phone, email, logo, default markup, sales tax, payment termsTo put your details and defaults on every documentYes, until you change or clear them. Stored under a one-way hash of your verified Clerk instance and user identifier. Your settings may themselves contain your business name and contact details.
Sign-in information and account identifierTo identify your account and keep each user's business settings separateYes. Clerk manages sign-in information, sessions, and OAuth grants. JobPaper uses your verified account identifier to select your settings.
Generated documentsSo you can reopen an estimate and make a change order from itNo. Documents are saved as files in your ChatGPT conversation, not on our servers.
PDFsTo send to your customerNo. PDFs are made on your device.

JobPaper does not collect your precise location, device address book, conversation history, or payment information. Clerk may use authentication cookies on its sign-in and account pages. JobPaper does not use advertising trackers in the document panel.

Who receives it

JobPaper does not sell or rent your data or use it for advertising or to train models. Vercel hosts the service, Upstash stores business settings, and Clerk handles sign-in and OAuth access. These providers process the information needed to perform those functions. ChatGPT itself is operated by OpenAI under OpenAI’s own privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Job text and photos are not retained. Business settings are kept until you clear them in JobPaper’s plugin settings or ask us to delete them at support@jobpaperapp.com; we delete them within 30 days of a request. Disconnecting the plugin stops new requests from that connection but does not delete your business settings or Clerk account. Contact us for account deletion.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Settings are stored encrypted at rest by our database provider, keyed by a hashed identifier.

Children

JobPaper is a business tool and isn’t directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date above. Material changes will be noted on this page.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: support@jobpaperapp.com.
